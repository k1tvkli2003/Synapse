## Frozen DNA

- Midnight clinical play: deep navy creates focus; cyan/cobalt supplies action; coral marks repair or urgency; warm gold marks earned mastery; warm paper belongs to evidence and trust.
- One calm task: every primary surface has one dominant action and a visible workload promise.
- Path before dashboard: a single five-stop learning route is more important than metrics, feeds, widgets, or feature cards.
- Recovery without shame: post-call protection, lighter review, reduced motion, stopping without penalty, and workload caps are product mechanics rather than decorative copy.
- Evidence as a contrasting material: citations and clinical provenance live on calm paper-like surfaces that remain readable and visually separate from game mechanics.
- Primary coach role: restored LUMA Character System. Its calm frost form, floating hands, high fold, inner bridge, low-wave return, and restrained cyan inner light are the selected acting and launcher language. ICR2-01 LUMA Facefront is its selected launcher portrait.
- Study and Clinical remain explicit modes: playful warmth is allowed in learning; clinical/reference workflows suppress reward noise and elevate provenance.
- Friendly typography, large rounded geometry, clear mastery rings, sparse luminous route lines, and restrained depth replace card-grid density.

## Binding Composition Rules

1. Today adapts to Tonight when schedule/context warrants; it is not a separate destination.
2. The curriculum hierarchy remains Course → Chapter → Unit → Micro-lesson even when contextual labels say On-Call Path, Calm Round, Review, or Shift Checkpoint.
3. Mobile shows three to five route nodes; larger screens may show context beside the route but never convert it into a dashboard wall.
4. Study screens may be dark; Evidence and high-density reading surfaces use a light or user-selectable reading material.
5. One dominant CTA per surface. Secondary actions are text, chips, drawers, or progressive disclosure.
6. Mastery outranks XP, ranks, streak length, and collectible value in visual hierarchy.
7. Mascots may coach, orient, celebrate, recover, or explain. They do not deliver clinical conclusions or obscure evidence.
8. Generated text, ECGs, anatomy, citations, or clinical claims are preview-only and never ship without validated live content.

The LUMA topology's documented association risk requires launch review rather than silent replacement.
- State matrix must include idle, prompt, hint, correct, repair, mastery, streak-safe, post-call, offline, error, reduced-motion, Clinical-suppressed, and accessibility fallback.
- Full, Essential, and Off density modes are mandatory. Clinical mode defaults to Essential or Off.
- Character names beyond LUMA remain working names until linguistic and trademark screening.

`$automate` is active for ordinary decisions inside this contract.
