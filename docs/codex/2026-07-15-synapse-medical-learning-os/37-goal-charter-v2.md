# Synapse Medical Learning OS — Goal Charter v3

**Status:** active execution authority  
**Updated:** 2026-07-22  
**Scope:** application, product system, shared data plane, capability absorption, content integration, and six-platform release truth  
**Atomic baseline:** 440-step WBS; subordinate evidence records may expand without an artificial cap

## 1. North-star outcome

Synapse becomes the most coherent, useful, memorable, and trustworthy Medical Learning OS for medical students, residents, and physicians: a Duolingo-grade medical curriculum at the center, surrounded by only those tools, workflows, evidence surfaces, personal knowledge capabilities, clinical reasoning modes, and community systems that tighten the learning-to-practice loop.

The target is not a collection of features and not a reskinned StudyHUB. It is one product with one identity, one navigation grammar, one data authority, one progression model, and multiple context-sensitive modes.

### Product promise

> Learn deeply. Recall reliably. Reason safely. Recover sustainably.

### Primary loop

1. **Orient:** Today prescribes the highest-value next action.
2. **Learn:** Path turns the curriculum into short, active, levelled journeys.
3. **Retrieve:** Recall schedules evidence-aware practice from actual performance.
4. **Reason:** Clinical mode trains cases, uncertainty, red flags, and differential reasoning.
5. **Ground:** Evidence connects answers to source, confidence, freshness, and educational boundaries.
6. **Create:** Workspace captures notes, documents, annotations, bookmarks, and personal synthesis in context.
7. **Reflect:** Progress makes mastery, gaps, time, wellbeing, and next steps legible.
8. **Belong:** Rounds/community adds safe, purposeful social learning without attention traps.

## 2. Priority and audience

- Medical education is the information-architecture spine and budget priority.
- Primary audiences are medical students, residents, and physicians; onboarding adapts intent, stage, available time, language, accessibility, and clinical schedule.
- Novices need confidence and guided progression; advanced users need density, precision, fast retrieval, and control. The same product must scale without exposing separate sub-apps.
- Education mode must never imply patient-specific medical advice. Clinical/reference surfaces require explicit provenance and safety boundaries.

The current execution budget is bindingly reweighted toward the learning core:

The immediate acceptance sequence is:

1. Browse a real-scale Course → Chapter → Unit → Concept Cluster → Micro-lesson path without losing source ordinals or locale context.
2. Enter a short, varied, feedback-rich Session and resume it exactly after interruption or restart.
3. Move between Guided Path and the contextual Study Workspace without duplicating content, progress, notes, or navigation.
4. Use the absorbed StudyHUB primitives inside the current chapter/micro-lesson/evidence context, with no legacy product language.
5. Prove English/Persian, RTL, keyboard/touch/pointer, 200% text, Full/Reduced/Off motion, offline/package recovery, and representative hierarchy performance.
6. Only then resume broad expansion of the remaining super-app portfolio.

Line count is an implementation inventory, not a completion metric. Throughput is measured by coherent learner jobs, executable states, verified data ownership, and real runtime evidence.

## 4. Preservation and absorption contract

### 4.1 No-loss rule

Every valid current Synapse behavior, route intent, deep link, persistence key, schema, user artifact, progress record, reward event, setting, and platform integration must be either:

- preserved unchanged;
- migrated deterministically with rollback and parity proof;
- absorbed into a stronger canonical workflow with fixture-backed equivalence; or
- explicitly retired only with user authorization and a recorded replacement/exit path.

### 4.2 StudyHUB absorption rule

Repository provenance is implementation metadata, never product language. Each imported capability is placed by user job:

- document reading and annotations live beside chapters, evidence, and workspace;
- notes, bookmarks, tags, and semantic links form a personal knowledge layer;
- SRS joins canonical Recall rather than creating another review system;
- planner and tasks become Today/Plan orchestration;
- AI jobs become secure, resumable, observable background work;
- widgets surface privacy-safe next actions and progress;
- legacy achievements map to canonical reward IDs without duplicate grants;
- dashboards contribute useful metrics/actions but do not survive as nested dashboards.

### 4.3 Page-sprawl rule

A standalone page is earned only when the capability has a sustained multi-step job, meaningful independent information architecture, deep-link value, and enough state to justify navigation. Otherwise use inline actions, contextual overlays, drawers, inspectors, command palette, background jobs, or workspace panes.

## 5. Curriculum and content contract

### 5.1 Stable identity

- IDs are derived from immutable source ordinals and versioned namespaces, not titles, translated labels, or mutable paths.
- Content releases are immutable; channels point to releases.
- Source hashes, atom dispositions, transformations, author/reviewer identity, model/prompt version, citations, and publication state are auditable.

### 5.2 Fine-grained learning

- A micro-lesson teaches one bounded objective using active participation, not a long scroll.
- A session is short enough for fatigue-sensitive use, normally no more than 8 minutes and 12 interactions per locale unless explicitly justified.
- Interaction types must be varied and medically meaningful: recognition, retrieval, ordering, matching, interpretation, localization, branching reasoning, confidence calibration, error correction, and case synthesis.
- Every distractor has a reviewed why-wrong explanation.
- Learning never depends on response speed; accessibility parity is mandatory.
- Embedded quizzes and bounded reinforcement summaries support the lesson. There is no separate Specialist/Enrich authoring pipeline.

### 5.3 Coverage and publication

- Every meaningful source atom receives `required`, `duplicate-required`, or `non-instructional` disposition with reason and provenance.
- Duplicate source bodies do not mint duplicate learner progress or rewards.
- Content remains quarantined until EN/FA parity, source coverage, schema, medical review, safety, and package-integrity gates pass.
- User-created source rights and privacy must be cleared before external Jules processing. No implicit upload is permitted.

## 7. Shared Data Plane

The Data Plane spans curriculum, progress, recall, cases, evidence, workspace, planner, AI, gamification, social, widgets, and settings.

### 7.1 Required layers

1. **Identity:** stable IDs, aliases, resource refs, user/device/session identity, and legacy mappings.
2. **Schema:** explicit versions, forward/backward migration rules, fixtures, and rollback.
3. **Provenance:** source, author, reviewer, timestamps, hashes, transform chain, evidence/citation, model/prompt versions.
4. **Validation:** structural, semantic, referential, locale-pair, safety, range, and integrity checks.
5. **Persistence:** local-first repositories with atomic writes and corruption detection.
6. **Indexing:** exact, fuzzy, semantic, filter, cross-locale, and personal-content search with deterministic fallbacks.
7. **Cache:** ownership, TTL/freshness, invalidation, quota, eviction, prefetch, and stale presentation.
8. **Offline:** queues, retries, idempotency keys, dependency ordering, cancellation, and user-visible state.
9. **Sync:** change capture, version vectors/revisions, tombstones, partial failure, and authoritative reconciliation.
10. **Conflict:** domain-specific auto-merge, user-assisted merge, audit trail, and lossless fallback copies.
11. **Privacy:** classification, minimization, consent, local/remote boundary, export, deletion, retention, and redaction.
12. **Security:** server authority, least privilege, row/object access, encryption posture, secret isolation, abuse/rate limits.
13. **Audit:** append-only event history where truth matters, correlation IDs, reason codes, and administrative evidence.
14. **Recovery:** backup, restore, reindex, cache rebuild, corrupt-record quarantine, migration rollback, and disaster runbooks.
15. **Observability:** privacy-safe metrics, traces, errors, sync health, content package health, and SLOs.

### 7.2 Truth boundaries

- Server authority: reward ledger, entitlements, shared/community state, high-risk AI jobs, moderation, publication channels.
- Local-first authority: drafts, reading state, transient session state, cached curriculum, offline annotations pending sync.
- Immutable package authority: published curriculum release bodies and localization pairs.
- Derived state: mastery, streak displays, dashboards, recommendations, and progress summaries; recomputable from authoritative events where practical.

## 8. Product-system requirements

### 8.1 Navigation and modes

- Preserve the five shell branch contracts while expressing Today, Path, Clinical, Rounds, and You.
- Universal search and command access cross curriculum, evidence, workspace, planner, and personal history without duplicating pages.
- Study mode is warm, encouraging, and gameful. Clinical/reference mode is calm, denser, provenance-forward, and minimally celebratory.
- Resume context survives restarts and deep links without trapping the user in a stale flow.

### 8.2 Gamification

- XP, mastery, streaks, levels, achievements, quests, currencies/energy if retained, and celebrations derive from idempotent domain events.
- Rewards reinforce learning quality and consistency, never unsafe speed, compulsive use, or false clinical confidence.
- Missed days, night shifts, illness, accessibility needs, and burnout risk receive humane recovery mechanics.
- The restored LUMA character acts as coach, evidence guide, quest host, and recovery companion through one coherent character bible and state machine.

### 8.3 Motion, sound, and haptics

- Major transitions and celebrations use authored storyboards/animatics and measured production techniques when simple tween code is insufficient.
- Motion communicates cause, continuity, hierarchy, and emotion; it never hides latency or blocks input.
- Full/Reduced/Off modes have equivalent meaning and completion, including mascot, reward, route, and data-state transitions.
- Sound and haptics are semantic, optional, platform-aware, and never the only feedback channel.

### 8.4 AI and evidence

- AI runs behind a secure gateway with scoped consent, redaction, quota, cancellation, resumability, citation capture, prompt/model audit, and explicit draft/review states.
- AI-generated medical content cannot publish itself.
- Evidence surfaces show source, date/freshness, confidence/quality, key takeaway, and education-not-patient-guidance boundary.

## 9. Quality attributes

### Accessibility

- WCAG-aligned semantics and contrast, platform accessibility APIs, screen-reader and keyboard parity, 200% text-scale resilience where applicable, reduced motion, color-independent meaning, and touch targets are release gates.

### Performance

- Startup, frame pacing, interaction latency, memory, package size, network, search, large-library, battery, and background-work budgets are defined by platform and calibrated on representative devices.
- No heavy visual, shader, mascot, or list implementation scales without profiler evidence and deterministic fallback.

### Reliability and errors

- Failures are typed, observable, retryable where safe, and translated into useful user actions.
- Background work is resumable and cancellation-aware.
- Destructive or privacy-sensitive operations include preview, confirmation, progress, receipt, and recovery path.

### Security and privacy

- No embedded service secrets, client-forgeable authoritative rewards, broad shared credentials, unredacted sensitive logs, or implicit uploads.
- Threat models cover authentication, authorization, local storage, sync, AI, content packages, community, widgets, deep links, and updates.

### Integrity

- Naming, routes, components, terminology, analytics, localization, state semantics, content identity, and platform surfaces share canonical registries.
- Legacy names stay in migration IDs only and never leak into user-facing strings.

## 10. Six-platform release truth

Each supported OS requires truthful evidence appropriate to its host:

- analyzer and deterministic tests;
- release build/package;
- install or served-artifact launch;
- navigation/deep-link and persistence smoke;
- real identity/icon/splash/taskbar/dock proof;
- EN/FA, RTL, text scale, keyboard/touch/pointer and accessibility proof;
- offline/resume/update/migration/error proof;
- representative performance trace;
- signing/update/entitlement/configuration review;
- artifact manifest, checksum, rollback and known-limitations record.

A passing Web build does not prove Android, desktop, or Apple runtime. A test suite does not prove a packaged release. Unsupported local hosts use CI or physical-host evidence and remain unverified until that evidence exists.

## 11. Ownership and parallel work

### Root application stream

Owns Flutter/domain code, shared Data Plane, preserved capability completion, UI/motion, backend contracts, platform delivery, content-package integration, and runtime proof.

### Content Lead stream

Owns corpus atomization, StudyHub prompt distillation, rights-gated Jules operation, EN/FA lesson/quiz/bounded-summary authoring, medical/content QA, coverage, and publication-ready artifacts. It must not edit application/domain files without an explicit handoff.

### Integration boundary

The application consumes versioned, validated content packages through frozen contracts. Root reviews the artifact and integrates it; it does not silently rewrite Content Lead bodies. Content Lead does not invent app schemas outside the contract.

## 14. Definition of complete

The Goal is complete only when all of the following are true:

- every WBS dependency and all discovered subordinate requirements are complete;
- 76/76 preserved capability records and every new capability are `verified` or explicitly `retired-authorized` by the user;
- the primary curriculum loop is complete with reviewed packages, fine-grained sessions, stable progress, Recall, evidence, and recovery;
- StudyHUB is fully absorbed with no user-facing sub-brand, duplicate authority, or lost user data;
- the shared Data Plane is implemented, migrated, observable, privacy-safe, offline-capable, recoverable, and tested;
- Night Shift, WMR2-01, the restored LUMA Character System, and ICR2-01 LUMA Facefront are consistently implemented with real visual evidence;
- EN and Persian/RTL are complete across product and content; accessibility parity is proven;
- Full/Reduced/Off motion and authored mascot/celebration systems are complete and profiled;

- backend, AI, rewards, community, content publication, security, privacy, performance, update, signing, observability, rollback, and recovery gates pass;

- the final `$perfect` loop finds no unresolved required issue.

Near-complete, scaffolded, compile-clean, test-green without runtime proof, or visually acceptable on one viewport/platform are not completion.
