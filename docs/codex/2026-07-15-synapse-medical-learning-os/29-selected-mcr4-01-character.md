## Why this supersedes Foldkin

The user identified the Round-3 anti-tooth result as an overcorrection: Foldkin and its peers read as objects rather than lovable characters. MCR4-01 restores character anatomy and acting range without restoring the rejected two-peak tooth silhouette.

- A clear oversized head, compact torso, attached arms/hands, legs, feet, posture, and center of gravity make it an actor rather than a decorated object.
- The single swept side crest and cyan living seam create recognition without a central crown valley, paired molar peaks, roots, or lower crossing.
- Large eyes, brows, mouth, hands, gaze, and whole-body poses support coaching, thinking, evidence, quest, recovery, and celebration jobs.
- The same character supports a close portrait launcher and a full-body in-app performance system.
- The Night Shift midnight/frost/cyan material and professional warmth remain unchanged.

## Locked character DNA

- One broad, softly rounded frost-white head with a single upper-left-to-upper-right swept crest; never split it into two peaks.
- One cyan side seam under the crest, strongest at the upper-left and tapering toward the face.
- Two large dark-navy glossy eyes with restrained highlights, expressive navy brows, and a small closed or lightly open mouth.
- Compact frost torso with a subtle cyan center seam and one small droplet-like chest light as a removable costume detail, not the identity source.
- Attached rounded arms, mitten hands, short legs, and grounded feet. Limbs must never float unless a storyboard explicitly uses an authored transition.
- Premium soft-touch frost material, controlled cobalt subsurface depth, minimal cyan emission, coral only for repair, and gold only for earned mastery.
- Mature friendliness for medical students, residents, and clinicians; never infantile, clinical-authoritative, mocking, or shame-inducing.
