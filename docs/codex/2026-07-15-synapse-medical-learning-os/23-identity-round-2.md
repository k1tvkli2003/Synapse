## Round-2 wordmark diagnosis

### Preserve from WM-02

- The fold is part of the letter, not decoration beside it.
- The word remains readable on first glance.
- Dimensional light can make the identity feel alive in rare hero moments.
- A flat one-color master can still preserve selected fold cuts.

### Correct

- Replace cold metallic/fashion-tech character with warm, confident, medical-learning friendliness.
- Establish one repeatable terminal/counter/fold grammar across all seven letters.
- Avoid making every letter perform the same trick; use hierarchy and restraint.
- Give `SYNAPSE` a memorable whole-word silhouette, not only interesting `N/A/P` details.
- Keep `S`, `Y`, and `E` equally authored so they do not look typed around three custom letters.
- Resolve `P` hook/question-mark risk and `N` legibility.
- Make the 24 px and one-color masters feel deliberately branded rather than stripped-down leftovers.
- Tie the fold logic to LUMA and learning progression without literal mascot-as-letter gimmicks.

## Visual and implementation gates

- Mascot boards may use LUMA's face and personality; abstract boards may not accidentally invent a second mascot.
- Symmetry means measured optical balance around a documented axis, not merely centering a shape.
- Asymmetry needs counterweight, stable visual center, and mask-safe balance; accidental lopsidedness fails.
- Each board shows a large unmasked master, flat/one-color, 256/96/48/24/16 px, circle/squircle/favicon, Apple light/dark/tinted, Android foreground/background/monochrome/safe-zone, and a bounded in-app motion storyboard plus final reduced/no-motion still.
- Launcher/store masters are static. Animated identity is an in-app splash/milestone consumer and never reward authority.
- Reject generic tech, progress rings, abstract `A`/play icons, road/automotive bridges, ears/cochleae, documents, AI sparkles, cold corporate geometry, and any copied Duolingo silhouette/trade dress.
- Every output is raster concept evidence only; selected marks and wordmarks require deterministic vector redraw, optical normalization, platform-native layers, small-size testing, and trademark/visual-similarity review.
