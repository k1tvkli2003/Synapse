# Brand and Mascot System — First-Pass Reference

> Status: the global theme, visual territory, logo metaphor, and mascot family are **not selected**. The first Atlas preview was rejected as dry and lifeless. This file preserves useful brand invariants and a superseded first pass; the active ten-direction exploration is `18-visual-concept-exploration.md`.

## Working Brand Architecture

- Master brand: Synapse.
- Product experience: selection pending; “Synapse Atlas” is one rejected visualized candidate, not the locked name/theme.
- Internal product metaphor: selection pending; map/atlas may remain a local curriculum pattern but cannot dictate the whole brand.
- Primary brand proposition: one connected system for learning, applying, retaining, and teaching medicine.
- Working positioning line: “Medicine, mapped to mastery.”
- Working support line: “Learn it. Apply it. Keep it.”

These lines are strategic working copy, not final shipped copy. Final English/Persian strings require product-writing, medical-language, linguistic, and legal/trademark review.

## Brand Idea

Medicine is not a pile of facts or a shelf of tools. It is a changing map of connections that becomes useful only when a person can retrieve, explain, apply, verify, and teach it. Synapse makes that map visible and gives every learning action a place in it.

## Brand Character

| Trait | Expression | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Brilliant but legible | Reveal complex relationships through hierarchy, scale, and progressive detail. | Dense cleverness, jargon display, mystery navigation. |
| Friendly but adult | Warm encouragement, humane recovery, original companions, clear next actions. | Childish toy-hospital language, shame, forced whimsy. |
| Clinically trustworthy | Provenance, version/date, uncertainty, calm states, separate clinical lens. | Confetti in critical contexts, fake certainty, decorative safety. |
| Ambitious but grounded | Lifelong mastery and cross-role continuity backed by real workflows. | “Perfect doctor,” superiority claims, unsupported outcomes. |
| Gameful but learning-first | Visible progress, meaningful challenges, cooperative goals, mastery-linked rewards. | Tap farming, pay-to-win, coercive streaks, loot boxes. |
| Global but language-aware | English source, Persian-native RTL, dual terminology, locale-neutral identity. | Flag-as-language, forced numerals/calendar, Latin-first layout patches. |

## Brand Principles

1. Make the next useful action obvious.
2. Make the reason behind adaptation visible.
3. Make progress mean something beyond completion.
4. Make mistakes feel diagnostic, not humiliating.
5. Make evidence and uncertainty part of the interface.
6. Make clinical moments quieter than study moments.
7. Make every artifact retain its lineage.
8. Make recovery as intentional as achievement.
9. Make characters serve cognition and emotion, never decoration alone.
10. Make the system recognizable without borrowing a competitor’s expression.

## Rejected First-Pass Visual Territory

The porcelain/graphite/editorial territory below is retained only as a record. It must not be treated as the implementation spec unless the user explicitly brings parts of it into the selected blend.

### Materials

- Porcelain paper: a warm, precise canvas that supports long reading without sterile white glare.
- Graphite edge: structural outlines, type, dividers, and map contour logic.
- Cobalt route: active educational path and focused interaction.
- Verdigris link: clinical/evidence relationship.
- Citrine signal: mastery, discovery, and positive attention.
- Restrained coral: warnings, error repair, and urgent contrast—not a dominant brand color.
- Cotton-fiber texture: subtle raster depth used only where it preserves readability/performance.

### Typography Direction

- English UI/editorial candidate: Manrope or another open variable sans with clear compact hierarchy.
- Persian candidate: Vazirmatn, separately tuned for line height, numerals, and mixed-script rhythm.
- Scientific/data candidate: IBM Plex Mono for bounded identifiers, doses, code-like data, and logs—not body copy.
- Long-form medical reading may use a separate highly legible text face only if bundle, language, and cross-platform metrics justify it.

Font selection is not final until license, glyph coverage, Arabic shaping, medical symbols, tabular numerals, variable-font support, fallback metrics, Android/iOS/Web rasterization, and performance tests pass.

### Geometry

- Asymmetrical tri-fold forms.
- Contour lines and nested scales.
- Ribbons for ordered sequences.
- Pins/seals for evidence and review state.
- Rings with labeled segments for mastery.
- Threads for causal/resource relationships.
- Rounded corners are selective and proportional; they do not turn every region into a card.

### Superseded Logo Direction

The working mark is an asymmetric three-plane Atlas Fold joined by one continuous seam. At small sizes it reads as a distinctive folded glyph; at large sizes the seam can imply connection/progression. It must not resolve into a brain, heart, S, neuron blob, book cliché, map pin, cross, caduceus, or Duolingo-like character.

Logo proof set:

- Black/white 16, 24, 32, 64, 128, and 512 px.
- Light/dark and one-color embroidery/print.
- App icon, favicon, notification icon, widget mark, splash, document export, watermark.
- English/Persian lockups with safe-area and small-size rules.
- Similarity and trademark review before lock.

## Mascot Strategy

Mascots are a cognition-and-relationship system. They guide, question, challenge, recover, and clarify. They never substitute for evidence, speak as clinicians, appear in critical alerts, or manipulate a user into unsafe engagement.

### First-Pass Family Candidate: Folioforms

Folioforms are not selected. Their role/safety/state thinking remains useful, but visual and naming decisions reopen after the user chooses the global world.

Original abstract living atlas folds—neither animals, humans, robots, organs, nor medical tools.

Shared design DNA:

- Asymmetrical tri-fold silhouette.
- Recessed charcoal negative-space face window.
- Graphite page edges.
- One luminous seam whose geometry carries state.
- Cotton-fiber porcelain material.
- Tiny integrated ribbon limbs, never toy-like gloves/shoes.
- Clear black silhouette at 64 px.
- Vector/Rive-capable base with optional bounded raster texture.

### Working Cast

| Character | Role | Silhouette | Behavioral boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nori | Primary companion and path guide | Three fan-fold planes with a bookmark tail | Curious, calm, persistent; never nags or pretends expertise. |
| Sera | Evidence/clinical guide | Taller squared two-fold base and precise seam | Highlights provenance, date, uncertainty; minimal emotion in Clinical. |
| Tavi | Quest and session host | Compact stacked-tab fold | Energetic and concise; never rewards passive engagement. |
| Rell | Friendly rival and challenge calibrator | Angular diamond fold | Direct but respectful; compares personal growth/skills, not human worth. |

Names are working names. Each requires pronunciation, negative-meaning, transliteration, locale, trademark, app-store, domain, and similarity review.

## Mascot State Matrix

| State | Trigger | Character behavior | Reduced-motion equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neutral | Today/Path idle | Nori open fold, near-static breathing | Static neutral pose |
| Encouraging | Session starts | Nori indicates the next route segment | Fixed highlight/gesture |
| Thinking | Response pending | Seam follows a short search route | Eye/fold state swap |
| Correct | Validated correct response | Brief seam alignment and light | Color/pose swap |
| Wrong/recovery | Incorrect response | Nori/Melo reveal an alternate route; no sadness performance | Static repair pose |
| Combo | Meaningful varied correct sequence | Fold planes align; no escalating screen takeover | Geometry badge only |
| Low capacity | Workload threshold | Melo offers rest, lighter review, or reschedule | Static low-capacity pose |
| Mastery change | Server-confirmed level evidence | Atlas ring completes behind Nori | Ring state swap |
| Continuity maintained | Sustainable daily/weekly target | Seam remains continuous | Static continuity glyph |
| On-call grace | Grace rule applies | Melo protects the continuity seam | Icon/state change |
| Quest complete | Authoritative quest completion | Tavi stamps route tabs once | Static seal |
| Reward summary | Transaction committed | Relevant cast appears only in one summary plate | Static group plate |
| Comeback | Return after absence | Melo opens a short route without backlog shame | Static welcome pose |
| Rest | Focus break/end | Fold closes with bookmark visible | Static rest pose |
| Offline/degraded | Sync unavailable | Nori shows a broken seam while offline work remains available | Status icon |
| Evidence caution | Stale/uncertain source | Sera highlights provenance/date | No character motion |
| Clinical critical | Safety/critical warning | Mascot removed entirely | None |

### User Control

Mascot visibility is a three-state preference:

- Full: study coaching, bounded celebrations, recovery, quests.
- Essential: navigation/help/recovery only.
- Off: no character visuals; all functions and feedback remain complete.

Clinical defaults to Essential or Off according to context. Accessibility/reduced-motion/data-saver preferences also constrain animation and raster loads.

Each asset manifest records:

- Stable asset ID.
- Character/role/state.
- Source prompt or source design file.
- Generator/model/version when applicable.
- Creation and approval dates.
- License/provenance.
- Light/dark/background constraints.
- LTR/RTL directionality.
- Motion/reduced-motion pair.
- Semantic description and alt behavior.
- Pixel/vector dimensions and safe area.
- Checksums and optimized variants.

No user-facing text is baked into mascot/UI raster assets. Real controls, medical copy, legal copy, doses, evidence, and user data stay live and semantic.

## No-Copy Checklist

- No owl, fox, bird, pet silhouette, or green mascot anchor.
- No stethoscope, caduceus, red cross, organ collectible, or brain character.
- No circular green lesson path, crowns, Duolingo-style hearts/gems/flame, or recognizable pose language.
- Continuity uses a seam, not a copied flame.
- No Finch-like dependent pet whose welfare pressures the user.
- No Forest-like death/failure guilt.
- No Pixar, Disney, or named living-artist imitation.
- No “Cortex”-like brain/blob silhouette or naming.
- No patient likeness, PHI, gore, or medical suffering in reward art.
- No disease/patient as enemy or loot.
- 64 px grayscale silhouette comparison against references passes.
- Reverse-image/similarity and trademark/linguistic review occur before production lock.
- Prompts, intermediate outputs, decisions, and checksums remain in the asset ledger.
