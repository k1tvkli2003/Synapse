# Duolingo-Medical Concept Reset

## User Correction

The prior boards were too crowded or too far from the core promise. They explored cities, cosmic biomes, optical labs, gardens, and graphic narratives instead of delivering a clean, delightful, character-led learning app that immediately reads as “Duolingo-grade medical education.” Those outputs are retained only as negative evidence.

## Shared Grammar for All Ten Rough Sparks

These began as rough sparks. Per the user’s latest direction, every option now receives its own high-fidelity visual preview before selection. Every option must use the same base:

- One dominant action per screen and generous whitespace.
- A simple chunky Course → Chapter → Lesson Path with only a few visible nodes.
- Large rounded controls, short friendly copy, immediate answer feedback, tactile motion, and reduced-motion parity.
- One iconic original primary mascot plus a small recurring cast; mascots are absent from critical Clinical states.
- Bright multi-color Study mode; calm evidence-first Clinical mode.
- Medical identity through path shapes, activity types, cases, evidence, terminology, illustration, and character roles—not dense dashboards or literal hospital decoration.
- Today / Path / Clinical / Rounds / You remains understandable within seconds.
- Original expression: no owl, green copied ladder, copied node geometry, brain/organ/stethoscope mascot, or competitor trade dress.

## Ten Focused Rough Sparks

### 1 — Pulse Pals

- Look: ultra-clean white/soft-navy UI, cobalt + aqua + coral + lemon, flat 2.5D shapes and very few elements.
- Path: one thick friendly pulse line; Chapter bends are milestones, Lesson nodes are rounded “beats,” Boss Cases are larger waveform gates.
- Mascot: **Pico**, an asymmetric pulse-shaped creature with a soft body and expressive face cutout; cast members represent Evidence, Recovery, Challenge, and Rounds.
- Brand feeling: energetic, instantly medical, simple, lovable.
- Risk: ECG motif could become repetitive; keep it as a path/motion signature, not decoration everywhere.

### 2 — Scrub Squad

- Look: bright sky blue, teal, lavender, coral and white; soft 3D/flat hybrid; friendly cast is the visual focus.
- Path: a clean rotation board with three to five big Lesson stops; Course/Chapter context stays above it.
- Mascot/cast: original non-human geometric characters wearing color-block “scrub” panels—coach, evidence expert, rival, recovery friend, rounds host.
- Brand feeling: closest to a character sitcom/friend group; community and belonging are strong.
- Risk: must avoid hospital-costume cliché and characters pretending to be clinicians.

### 3 — MedBits

- Look: crisp white, cobalt, lime, coral, violet; modular rounded pieces that click together with satisfying motion.
- Path: lessons are big “bits”; mastering and applying them connects the bits into a visible pathway, never an organ.
- Mascot/cast: small asymmetric link-creatures made of two or three interlocking forms, each with a distinct silhouette.
- Brand feeling: iconic, toy-like enough to be fun, technical enough to feel medical/scientific.
- Risk: can look like a generic puzzle app unless medical activity art and evidence states are strong.

### 4 — Vital Trail

- Look: clean off-white UI with cobalt, mint, coral, gold; simple illustrated medical-system scenery only around the Path.
- Path: a friendly winding vital-signal trail across compact Course regions; never a full 3D world and never dense.
- Mascot: **Vivi**, a soft geometric guide carrying a tiny route marker; no anatomical shape.
- Brand feeling: adventurous but still unmistakably a simple learning app.
- Risk: must not drift back into the rejected atlas/world-map density.

### 5 — Clinic Crew

- Look: airy white, blue, mint and orange; big rounded panels; playful contemporary clinic/school cues without patient imagery.
- Path: each Chapter has a simple “station” strip; Lessons are three or four large friendly doors/instruments on screen.
- Mascot/cast: abstract crew with role-colored badges—Study Coach, Evidence Keeper, Case Challenger, Recovery Buddy.
- Brand feeling: warm, safe, highly approachable and directly medical.
- Risk: could become generic healthcare branding; mascot silhouette and humor must carry distinctiveness.

### 6 — WonderLab

- Look: very bright white with cyan, yellow, violet, coral; soft tactile controls and tiny controlled science animations.
- Path: a clean experiment bench with large Lesson modules; each activity “tests” one concept and gives instant visual feedback.
- Mascot: **Bop**, an asymmetric reaction-spark character, plus a calm evidence character and energetic quest character.
- Brand feeling: playful discovery and hands-on learning, especially strong for ECG/labs/sounds/calculators.
- Risk: keep tool imagery secondary so the platform still centers curriculum rather than a lab simulator.

### 7 — Med League

- Look: sporty clean navy/white with sky, coral and gold; bold numerals, chunky progress rings, no visual clutter.
- Path: clear training track with Lesson drills, Checkpoints, Boss Cases and team Rounds; mastery outranks XP.
- Mascot/cast: original abstract team captains with expressive shapes, not human athletes or animals.
- Brand feeling: high-energy, competitive, motivational and social.
- Risk: requires humane leagues, opt-out, on-call grace, and no shame/competence conflation.

### 8 — Anatomy Pop

- Look: clean white UI plus bold flat medical illustration plates in cobalt, coral, mint, violet and yellow.
- Path: Lesson nodes sit on simplified educational diagrams of systems/pathways, with only one diagram and three to five nodes visible.
- Mascot: **Mori**, an abstract pointer/guide character that interacts with illustrations but is not an organ.
- Brand feeling: the most explicitly medical and visually teachable option.
- Risk: illustrations need strict accuracy, localization-safe labels, and graceful subjects where anatomy is not the right metaphor.

### 9 — Night Shift

- Look: deep navy background, bright cyan/coral/yellow path, large rounded surfaces, minimal glow, very clean hierarchy.
- Path: a luminous but simple three-node trail optimized for residents studying after shifts; Today emphasizes a compact adaptive workload.
- Mascot/cast: softly lit abstract companions with calm humor; Recovery character is unusually important.
- Brand feeling: cool, premium, modern and mature while still gameful.
- Risk: needs a first-class bright theme and excellent contrast; must not become a sci-fi dashboard.

### 10 — Synapse Core

- Look: the closest structural answer to the brief—bright white/ink base, cobalt as primary, mint/coral/gold accents, chunky rounded nodes, friendly 2.5D illustration, lots of empty space.
- Path: a simple centered Course → Chapter → Lesson trail with mastery rings, review forks, Checkpoint and Boss Case nodes; original geometry and no copied green ladder.
- Mascot/cast: a strong original “Synapsian” family with one primary coach, evidence guide, quest host, rival, and recovery companion.
- Brand feeling: the clearest “Duolingo-grade medical learning” foundation with the lowest conceptual noise.
- Risk: originality must come from mascot world, medical activity language, path behavior, sound/motion and Clinical lens—not by merely reskinning a familiar app.
