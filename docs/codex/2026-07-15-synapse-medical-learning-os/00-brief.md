## Product Promise

Synapse should help a medical student, resident, or physician answer five questions with minimal navigation:

1. What is the highest-value thing for me to learn or review now?
2. Where am I in the medical curriculum and why is this next?
3. Can I recall, explain, connect, diagnose, decide, defend, teach, and transfer this concept?
4. What trustworthy evidence, notes, media, tools, and clinical context support it?
5. How do I stay consistent without unsafe pressure, shallow point farming, or professional compromise?
