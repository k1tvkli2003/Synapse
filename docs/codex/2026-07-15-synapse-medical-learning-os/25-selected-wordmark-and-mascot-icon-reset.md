At this checkpoint the user withdrew ICR2-01 because the mascot and icon can both be read as a tooth. The later direct restoration supersedes that selection veto while retaining the concern as a required association and real-host launch-review gate. The approved Night Shift world never changed.

### Preserve

- Deep midnight-navy field.
- Frost/ice primary material with restrained cobalt/cyan inner light.
- Soft dimensional volume, subtle subsurface glow, and premium calm rendering.
- Tiny dark eyes, restrained closed smile, mature friendliness, and shame-free support behavior.
- A character that can become the app icon, coach, quest host, recovery companion, and celebration lead without becoming a clinical authority.

### Reject at silhouette level

- Two top peaks separated by a central valley.
- Molar crown/cusp structure, paired roots, or a lower `X`/root crossing.
- Enamel-white tooth proportions, even if recolored or given a face.
- Cat-ear, ghost-sheet, pillow, folded tissue, organ, pill/capsule, shield, medical cross, brain, ECG, AI sparkle, owl, or copied learning-app trade dress.
- A generic blob whose identity depends only on eyes, gradients, or glow.
