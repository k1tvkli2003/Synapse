## Historical association concern retained for launch review

- The high left/right folds create a crown/cusp reading.
- The frost-white enamel-like material reinforces dental association.
- The lower bridge/crossing can read as roots or a molar base.
- The same ambiguity survives silhouette, monochrome, and small-size views, so color or facial adjustments cannot repair it.
- This was previously treated as a category-level silhouette defect. The user's later restoration means it is now a research and release-review risk, not a replacement instruction.

## Historical correction notes

- Make one high fold subtly dominant so the top does not read as equal cat ears.
- Convert the lower crossing into one clean inner-bridge/low-wave return rather than legs or an `X`.
- Slightly reduce eye size and raise the face for a more mature professional expression.
- Author separate hero, launcher, compact, monochrome, and 16 px optical masters.
- Use crisp flat geometry first; add dimensional light as a controlled surface treatment, not the only source of identity.

These notes record the interim critique. The current package uses the exact selected LUMA face and preserves its high fold, inner bridge, low-wave return, calm face, frost material, and cyan light rather than substituting a different topology.
