# Synapse chapter atom review and decomposition

## Outcome

Produce one chapter-scoped decomposition draft and coverage ledger from the
exact task envelope. This phase does not write learner-visible lesson bodies.

## Binding inputs

The activated task must provide immutable repository-relative paths and hashes
for:

- the approved source-scan fragment;
- every chapter source document and media record;
- `content-contract.v1.json`;
- `authoring-policy.v1.json`;
- the exact output namespace owned by this task.

Stop with a structured blocking finding if any required path, hash, source atom,
or policy is absent or inconsistent. Do not infer missing source text and do not
execute, import, transpile, or evaluate source-side TypeScript.

## Required work

1. Reconcile every assigned source document and atom exactly.
2. Review `required`, `duplicate_required`, and `non_instructional`
   dispositions; keep every source identity even when content is duplicated.
3. Build claim candidates with risk, temporality, jurisdiction, evidence
   requirement, source-atom links, and conflict state.
4. Propose the complete fine-grained topology:
   `Chapter → Unit → Concept Cluster → Micro-lesson → Session → Interaction`.
5. Use source Lesson directories and headings as boundary evidence, never as
   automatic learner-facing lesson boundaries.
6. Split until every proposed session can satisfy both English and Persian
   duration, concept, claim, interaction, active-step, and monotony budgets.
7. Preserve prerequisites, prior/next continuity, split rationale, duplicate
   aliases, media purpose, and source-atom ranges.
8. Identify mechanism-heavy, image or signal interpretation, management, and
   safety clusters explicitly.

## Forbidden actions

- Do not author lesson prose, questions, summaries, or translations.
- Do not create a specialist or Enrich stage.
- Do not treat a recap or derived sidecar as primary coverage authority.
- Do not fabricate a claim, citation, media meaning, or missing source.
- Do not change shared registries, another chapter, application code, branches,
  commits, pull requests, or publication channels.
- Do not approve your own output.

## Output and acceptance

Write only the assigned decomposition and coverage-ledger paths. The result must
be deterministic, source-root-free, schema-valid, and include:

- 100% document and atom reconciliation;
- zero unclassified or silently dropped atoms;
- explicit duplicate and non-instructional accounting;
- proposed stable identities and source lineage;
- unresolved rights, evidence, medical, media, and ambiguity findings;
- no learner-visible bodies and no promotion state above `raw`.
